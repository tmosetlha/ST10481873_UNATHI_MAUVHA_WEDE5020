About GreenRoots NPO

Our story, our people, our purpose.

Our History

GreenRoots NPO was founded in 2026 by a group of passionate Johannesburg residents who believed that unused urban spaces could be transformed into vibrant, productive community gardens. What began as a single plot in Braamfontein has grown into a network of 12 active gardens across Gauteng.

Over the past eight years, GreenRoots has partnered with local municipalities, schools, and corporates to deliver impactful environmental education programmes, food security initiatives, and green space development projects throughout the city.

In 2022, we received the prestigious Gauteng Environmental Excellence Award for our contributions to urban greening and community upliftment, cementing our reputation as a leading force in sustainable community development.

Our Mission

To transform underutilised urban land into thriving community gardens that promote food security, environmental sustainability, and social cohesion across Johannesburg's communities.

Our Vision

A Johannesburg where every community has access to green, productive shared spaces that nourish both people and the planet — a city rooted in sustainability and collective well-being.

Our Core Values

  • Sustainability: We practise and promote environmentally responsible methods in everything we do.
  • Community First: Every decision we make centres on the needs and voices of the communities we serve.
  • Inclusivity: Our gardens and programmes are open and accessible to people of all ages, backgrounds, and abilities.
  • Education: We believe knowledge is a seed — we plant it in people, and it grows into change.
  • Transparency: We operate with full financial and operational transparency to maintain public trust.
  • Collaboration: We partner with governments, schools, businesses, and individuals to maximise our collective impact.
