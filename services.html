Our Programmes

Planting seeds of change across Johannesburg's communities.

What We Offer

GreenRoots NPO runs a diverse range of programmes designed to transform communities through sustainable urban gardening, environmental education, and green skills development. All our programmes are free of charge to community members and schools.

1. Community Garden Establishment

We identify underused urban spaces and, working with local government and property owners, transform them into thriving community gardens. Each garden is managed by a trained local committee with ongoing support from GreenRoots.

What's Included:

  • Site assessment and soil preparation
  • Design and layout of raised garden beds
  • Supply of seeds, seedlings, and tools
  • Installation of water-efficient irrigation systems
  • Ongoing mentorship for local garden committees

Duration: Permanent installation | Locations: 12 sites across Gauteng

2. Environmental Education Programme

Our education team visits schools and community centres to deliver engaging, curriculum-aligned environmental education sessions. We make learning about nature fun, practical, and impactful for learners of all ages.

Topics Covered:

  • Introduction to sustainable agriculture
  • Biodiversity and ecosystem services
  • Water conservation and responsible usage
  • Climate change and what we can do locally
  • Food security and nutrition basics

Target Group: Grades 4–12, Community Adults | Duration: 1–2 hour sessions

3. Composting Workshops

Our Saturday composting workshops teach community members how to recycle organic waste into nutrient-rich compost, reducing landfill waste while enriching garden soil. These sessions are hands-on and practical.

Workshop Highlights:

  • Understanding organic vs inorganic waste
  • Building a home composting bin from scratch
  • What to compost and what to avoid
  • Troubleshooting common composting problems
  • Using finished compost in your garden

Schedule: Every Saturday, 09:00–11:00 | Venue: Rotating across garden sites

4. Corporate Greening Partnerships

We partner with companies to fulfil their Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) goals through meaningful, hands-on environmental projects. Corporate partners can sponsor garden sites, fund equipment, or bring teams to volunteer alongside our community members.

Partnership Options:

  • Garden Sponsorship (naming rights available)
  • Employee Volunteer Days
  • Seed and Equipment Donations
  • Education Programme Funding
  • Annual Corporate CSR Reports provided

For enquiries: Submit a Partnership Enquiry
